## Featured Project

### [Coral Reef Stress & Bleaching Monitor](https://github.com/tugbatuanakaygisiz-web/coral_simulation)

[Live demo](https://tugbatuanakaygisiz-web.github.io/coral_simulation/) · [Source code](https://github.com/tugbatuanakaygisiz-web/coral_simulation)

An interactive simulation of how Pacific coral reefs respond to marine heatwaves and emissions policy, projected to 2100 under the IPCC SSP2-4.5 scenario.

<a href="https://tugbatuanakaygisiz-web.github.io/coral_simulation/"><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tugbatuanakaygisiz-web/coral_simulation/main/docs/screenshot-dark.jpg" alt="Coral Reef Stress & Bleaching Monitor" width="720"></a>

- Built on approximately 269,000 NOAA coral colony observations
- Calibrated against field surveys, reducing error from 13.0 to 5.2 percentage points
- Vanilla JavaScript, HTML5 Canvas and Chart.js; bilingual interface, reproducible runs
